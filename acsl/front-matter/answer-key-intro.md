# Using This Answer Key

This book gives worked answers to the odd-numbered exercises in every unit.
The answers are grouped by unit, in the same order as the exercises.

## Try first, compare after

Work each exercise before you open this book.
If you get stuck, read just enough of the answer to spot your next step, close the book, and finish the item yourself.
When you are done, compare your work with the answer and ask why any differences matter.

## Reading an answer

A short-answer item shows the worked method, then a line such as **Answer:** `10110`, the one correct form of the answer.
Your answer is right only when it matches that form exactly: the same digits, spacing and order.
If your method was different but your answer matches, your method may be right too; check it on a second example.

A programming answer is a complete program that reads its input with `input()`.
Run it with a sample input file, for example `python solution.py < sample.txt`, and compare its output with the sample output.

## Why only the odd-numbered exercises

The even-numbered exercises are for checking yourself: use the odd-numbered answers to learn a method, then prove you can use it on the even-numbered ones.
The four practice papers are not in this book for the same reason; they measure what you can do on your own, under a clock.
