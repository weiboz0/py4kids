## Why the capstone needs a Unit-14 *integration unit* (tool-forced)
`tools/curriculum.py practice_findings` enforces the capstone rule: once `projects/<capstone>/` exists, every concept
must already be **practised by a non-capstone entry**.

Eight concepts are introduced in Units 9–12 but practised by **no** later entry:

| Introduced in | Concepts |
|---|---|
| U9 | `feature-towers`, `hard-negatives` |
| U10 | `ann-retrieval`, `hnsw` |
| U11 | `neural-reranking`, `ranking-features`, `learning-to-rank` |
| U12 | `self-attention` |

Checkpoint B deliberately skipped these, as the heavy fits. A capstone-only U14 would therefore **fail CI**.

So U14 has two entries:
- **`unit-14-full-system`** (a unit, `lessons: 3`, `introduces: []`). It assembles the whole Part-2 stack and
  **practises those eight concepts**, plus the rest of the pipeline.
- **The capstone project `project-14-bookrec`** (`kind: project`, `lessons: 3`). The student builds, measures and
  defends their own full recommender.

This mirrors `python-projects`, where projects are separate from units. Design §8 row 14 is amended to say so.

## Scope

### Unit 14 (`recsys/units/unit-14-full-system/`)
The project-first hook: "You have built twelve kinds of recommender. Now build *the* recommender, one system,
end to end, and decide what earns its place."

The lesson assembles, on the main log:
1. **Retrieval:**
   - popularity, lexical/BM25, item-item, MF, semantic;
   - the two-tower **served through an HNSW ANN index** (U10; exact-vs-ANN recall check);
   - the **feature tower** for cold items (U9), served as shipped with `negative_pool="warm"` (0.298, cold
     coverage 0.138). **Hard negatives are practised as an explicit ablation**: switch the pool to `"hard"` and see
     the measured warm↔cold trade (warm 0.172 / cold coverage 0.226; recsys-011). They are not part of the served
     stack;
   - the U10 hybrid.
2. **Candidate pool + blend:** the calibrated union (U6/U10).
3. **The learned reranker** (U11), with the leakage-safe time-ordered holdout, re-scoring the pool from **ranking
   features**: **learning to rank**.
4. **The sequence path on the session log** (U12): **self-attention** (SASRec), plus last-k/transition. It is scored
   separately, per design §6.
5. **The whole-system readout:**
   - an **ablation table** (stage rows vs the full stack; path rows as **fixed-blend leave-one-out**, not reranked);
   - a **beyond-accuracy table** (coverage, head-share, the exposure Gini, novelty/diversity; U6/U13);
   - the benchmark vs the **Part-1 build** (the U6/Checkpoint A blend).

It runs **val only**. `test` is not unsealed again (the two authorized unseals were CP-A and CP-B), and the global
AST allowlist is unchanged.

### Cached models (design §8 row 14 requirement, scoped to U14 + the capstone only)
A seeded script `recsys/projects/bookrec/scripts/build_models.py` fits the expensive models once and writes their
torch-free `artifact()`s to `recsys/data/generated/models/` (gitignored, regenerated-only, like the data):
- MF, the two-tower, the feature tower, the HNSW index, the reranker, SASRec on the session log.

The wiring:
- `scripts/ci-local.sh` runs it in the routed recsys step, after data generation.
- The U14 lesson/solutions/milestone and the capstone notebooks **load** the artifacts with `path.load(...)` instead
  of refitting. **Exception:** the U14 lesson fits **MF, the two-tower and HNSW once on screen** and shows the
  loaded cache is equivalent. The feature tower, both rerankers and SASRec load from the cache with their stored
  `loss_history`; students saw those fits performed in U9, U11 and U12.
- **The required check** (`tests/test_unit14.py`) asserts several things:
  - every artifact exists, matches its schema and version, and is torch-free to load;
  - each loaded path reproduces its pinned val hit@10 (rank/tolerance-based, §184);
  - a regeneration in the test **equals** the cached artifact for the cheap models, and the expensive ones are
    checked by rank-overlap against their pinned top-k on a fixed reader sample.

## Reranker provenance (v2; [sol] r1 blocker)
`build_models.py` freezes the **U11 shipped leakage-safe recipe** exactly:
- train-only rows;
- per reader, the time-ordered **latest 25% of train positives (≥ 1) = labels** and the earlier 75% = profile;
- the feature paths are **refit on the profile rows** (excluding every occurrence of a held-out label item);
- `log_pop` and the genre/author statistics come from the profile;
- serving uses the full-train paths.
It calls `NeuralRerankerPath` with the same `path_factory` / `assemble_training_matrix` as U11; nothing is
re-implemented.

Required provenance tests in `test_unit14.py`:
1. **val/test mutation invariance.** On a **reduced config**, an **unmutated reduced build** and a **mutated
   reduced build** (val/test rows dropped or relabelled) go through the identical `build_models` code path, and the
   two must produce `allclose` reranker artifacts. The comparison is reduced vs reduced, never against the
   production cache.
2. **No held-out label event enters features or statistics.** U11's regression test (the repeat-read `log_pop`
   case) is re-asserted on the build script's assembly call.
3. **The cache records its recipe:** the artifact metadata carries `recipe = "u11-holdout-75-25"` and the profile
   fraction, and the test asserts them.

    - The feature tower, reranker and SASRec are verified by the pinned val hit plus the 20-reader oracle, which

## The assembled system — pinned definition (v3; [sol] r2 B1)
**The full stack:**
1. **Retrieval:** an equal-weight **8-path pool** with pool size 50 and k = 10. The 8 paths are popularity,
   lexical, item-item, MF, semantic, the two-tower **served through HNSW**, the feature tower (warm) and the U10
   hybrid.
2. **Blend:** calibrated union (`blend`), excluding seen books.
3. **Re-rank (v4; [sol] r3 B1):** an **8-path learned reranker** trained with the **unchanged U11 recipe**,
   generalised to 8 paths through the shipped API. The configuration is:
   ```
   NeuralRerankerPath(full8, fit_eight, catalog, pool=50, seed=0,
                      path_names=(popularity, lexical, item-item, mf, semantic, two-tower,
                                  feature-tower, hybrid))
   ```
   with `assemble_training_matrix(rows)` (the time-ordered 75/25 profile/label holdout, profile-refit paths and
   statistics), followed by `set_model(RerankerModel(seed=0).fit(X, y))`. This is exactly the probe's recipe
   (`scratchpad/fable017/probe.py` lines 325–341). The features number 21 = 8 calibrated scores + 8 presence flags
   + `n_paths` + 4 content features. At serving the two-tower is served through HNSW.

There are **two separate cached reranker artifacts**:
- `reranker-u11` is the shipped 6-path U11 model (fidelity pin 0.346);
- `reranker-full8` is the full-stack model above.
The provenance tests apply to **both**. Measured by
[fable]'s probe (r1):

| System | hit@10 | coverage | head-share | item Gini | novelty | diversity |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Full stack (8-path pool + MLP reranker, ANN)** | **0.356** | 0.203 | 0.847 | 0.913 | 8.57 | 0.857 |
| Stage ablation: no reranker (fixed 8-path blend, ANN) | 0.362 | 0.206 | 0.846 | 0.923 | 8.43 | 0.855 |
| Stage ablation: exact two-tower (no ANN), reranked | 0.354 | 0.203 | — | — | — | — |
| Reference: dense-heavy fixed weights | 0.376 | 0.184 | 0.885 | — | — | — |
| Reference: content-only reranker | 0.384 | 0.137 | 0.960 | — | — | — |
| Reference: U10 hybrid (best single path) | 0.362 | 0.192 | — | — | — | — |
| Reference: Part-1 U6 blend | 0.306 | 0.333 | — | — | — | — |

**The honest thesis:** the learned reranker over the full pool *dilutes*. It scores 0.356, below the fixed blend's
0.362, as U11 found. The only hit gains (dense-heavy weights, content-only or linear reranking) buy hit with
coverage and concentration; no system dominates the Part-1 blend on reach (0.333). The capstone's deliverable is
the **hit ↔ reach ↔ exposure frontier** and a defended choice on it, not "the full stack wins".

within ±0.003**. The
fixed 8-path blend reproduces 0.362 / 0.206 within ±0.003.

## Budget (binding ceilings, loaded)

  - **U14 lesson ≤ 4 min:** on-screen fits of MF, the two-tower and HNSW only; the rest loads from the cache. The
    lesson and the capstone solutions **read the ablation and beyond-accuracy rows from `readouts.json`**, and
    recompute live only what they teach (the full stack, the fixed blend and Part-1). The hard-negative ablation
    loads `feature-tower-hard` from the cache.
  - **U14 solutions ≤ 2.5 min.**
  - **Capstone solutions ≤ 3 min.**
  - **Notebook total ≤ 15 min.**
  - **Routed pytest ≤ +3 min.** That covers the MF and two-tower refit `allclose`, the oracle, the per-model val
    pins, the two reduced mutation builds and the `readouts.json` structure.
- **Lesson on-screen fits (v3, pre-declared):** only MF, the two-tower and the HNSW build are fitted on screen
  (≈ 35 s plus 0.2 s). The feature tower, reranker and SASRec load from the cache. Phase B adds an additive

  U9, U11 and U12.
- **Cache facts ([fable] r1):**
  - the pickle format (protocol 5) is about 2 MB in total;
  - the reranker artifact loads against the same fitted 6 serving paths (cheap refits plus the cached MF and
    two-tower);
  - `AnnRetrievalPath.load` **rebuilds** HNSW from the stored vectors (0.2 s), so "cache the ANN" means "cache the
    two-tower and rebuild";
  - `load_cached_models(dir)` raises a clear `FileNotFoundError` naming the build command.
  - A `manifest.json` sidecar records the `bookrec` artifact versions plus the sha256 of `checksums.json` for the
    generated data. `load_cached_models` refuses a cache whose data hash no longer matches, so a regenerated
    dataset invalidates the cache.
  - The amendment names `recsys/data/generated/models/` (gitignored, regenerated-only) as the cache location, not
    `assets/`.
