the **final** plan of Book 3.

## Why the capstone needs a Unit-14 *integration unit* (tool-forced)
`tools/curriculum.py practice_findings` enforces the capstone rule: once `projects/<capstone>/` exists, every concept
must already be **practised by a non-capstone entry**.

Eight concepts are introduced in Units 9–12 but practised by **no** later entry:

| Introduced in | Concepts |
|---|---|
| U9 | `feature-towers`, `hard-negatives` |
| U10 | `ann-retrieval`, `hnsw` |
| U11 | `neural-reranking`, `ranking-features`, `learning-to-rank` |
| U12 | `self-attention` |

Checkpoint B deliberately skipped these, as the heavy fits. A capstone-only U14 would therefore **fail CI**.

So U14 has two entries:
- **`unit-14-full-system`** (a unit, `lessons: 3`, `introduces: []`). It assembles the whole Part-2 stack and
  **practises those eight concepts**, plus the rest of the pipeline.
- **The capstone project `capstone-bookrec`** (`kind: project`, `lessons: 3`). The student builds, measures and
  defends their own full recommender.

This mirrors `python-projects`, where projects are separate from units. Design §8 row 14 is amended to say so.

## Scope

### Unit 14 (`recsys/units/unit-14-full-system/`)
The project-first hook: "You have built twelve kinds of recommender. Now build *the* recommender, one system,
end to end, and decide what earns its place."

The lesson assembles, on the main log:
1. **Retrieval:**
   - popularity, lexical/BM25, item-item, MF, semantic;
   - the two-tower **served through an HNSW ANN index** (U10; exact-vs-ANN recall check);
   - the **feature tower with hard negatives** for cold items (U9);
   - the U10 hybrid.
2. **Candidate pool + blend:** the calibrated union (U6/U10).
3. **The learned reranker** (U11), with the leakage-safe time-ordered holdout, re-scoring the pool from **ranking
   features**: **learning to rank**.
4. **The sequence path on the session log** (U12): **self-attention** (SASRec), plus last-k/transition. It is scored
   separately, per design §6.
5. **The whole-system readout:**
   - an **ablation table** (leave-one-stage/path-out);
   - a **beyond-accuracy table** (coverage, head-share, the exposure Gini, novelty/diversity; U6/U13);
   - the benchmark vs the **Part-1 build** (the U6/Checkpoint A blend).

It runs **val only**. `test` is not unsealed again (the two authorized unseals were CP-A and CP-B), and the global
AST allowlist is unchanged.

### Capstone (`recsys/projects/capstone-bookrec/`)
Files: `brief.ipynb`, `solutions.ipynb`, `teacher-notes.md`, `manifest.yaml`. The brief's milestones are:
1. pick and justify retrieval paths;
2. build the pool + blend;
3. add the reranker;
4. run the ablation;
5. produce the beyond-accuracy table;
6. benchmark vs Part 1;
7. write a short design memo on the trade-offs (accuracy vs reach vs exposure; cold start; cost).
It is val only. It is project-strict: no borrowed tools, everything taught.

### Cached models (design §8 row 14 requirement, scoped to U14 + the capstone only)
A seeded script `recsys/projects/bookrec/scripts/build_models.py` fits the expensive models once and writes their
torch-free `artifact()`s to `recsys/data/generated/models/` (gitignored, regenerated-only, like the data):
- MF, the two-tower, the feature tower, the HNSW index, the reranker, SASRec on the session log.

The wiring:
- `scripts/ci-local.sh` runs it in the routed recsys step, after data generation.
- The U14 lesson/solutions/milestone and the capstone notebooks **load** the artifacts with `path.load(...)` instead
  of refitting. **Exception:** the U14 lesson performs each fit **once on screen** (taught-before-assessed: students
  see the fit) and then shows that the loaded cache is equivalent.
- **The required check** (`tests/test_unit14.py`) asserts several things:
  - every artifact exists, matches its schema and version, and is torch-free to load;
  - each loaded path reproduces its pinned val hit@10 (rank/tolerance-based, §184);
  - a regeneration in the test **equals** the cached artifact for the cheap models, and the expensive ones are
    checked by rank-overlap against their pinned top-k on a fixed reader sample.

## Gates (pinned by the plan-gate probe; re-measured in Phase B; a miss means stop, amend and re-gate)
Main log, val, k = 10, committed seed:
- **(a) The assembled system is coherent:**
  - every path loaded from the cache reproduces its unit's pinned val hit@10 within ±0.003, for example two-tower
    0.340, hybrid 0.362, feature tower 0.298 / cold coverage, and reranker 0.346;
  - ANN recall@10 vs exact ≥ 0.99.
- **(b) The ablation table is honest and complete:** one row per stage/path removed, with signed Δhit and Δcoverage.
  It is **recorded, not gated on direction**; the plan does not pre-assume the full stack wins.
- **(c) The beyond-accuracy table:** coverage, head-share, Gini, novelty and diversity for the full system, the
  best single path and the Part-1 build. It is recorded.
- **(d) The cache required check passes**, and the U14 notebooks perform zero refits after the lesson's one-time
  demonstrations.
- **(e) The Part-1 benchmark:** the Part-1 build (the U6 blend) on val is 0.306 / 0.333, and the full system's
  numbers are reported against it.

## Budget (binding ceilings, loaded)
- **`build_models.py`:** ≤ 5 min. Probe-measured, roughly MF 17 s + two-tower 18 s + feature tower 26 s + reranker
  about 90 s + SASRec about 50 s + HNSW about 5 s.
- **U14 lesson:** ≤ 4 min (one on-screen fit of each heavy model, ≤ 90 s per cell, plus cache loads).
- **U14 solutions:** ≤ 2.5 min (cache loads only).
- **U14 milestone:** ≤ 2.5 min (cache loads only).
- **Capstone solutions:** ≤ 3 min (cache loads only).
- **Total:** ≤ 15 min of notebook exec, plus the build script inside the routed pytest step.
- **Routed pytest:** ≤ +90 s, including the required check (it uses the CI-built cache and does not refit the
  expensive models).
- **If a ceiling is exceeded:** first drop the lesson's on-screen refits of the slowest model (show its loss curve
  from the cached artifact metadata); otherwise stop and amend.

## Concepts / registry
- **No new concepts** (both U14 entries introduce `[]`); the lessons total goes from 40 to 46.
- **`unit-14-full-system`:**
  - `requires` / `practices` include the 8 never-practised concepts above, plus the pipeline concepts:
    `retrieve-then-rank`, `offline-evaluation`, `top-k-ranking-metrics`, `score-blending`, `beyond-accuracy`,
    `two-tower`, `embedding-retrieval`, `hybrid-retrieval`, `item-cold-start`, `sequential-recommendation`,
    `exposure-fairness`, and so on.
  - Every `practices` id must already be introduced. `practices ∩ introduces = ∅` holds trivially.
- **`capstone-bookrec`** (`kind: project`, the last map entry):
  - `requires` = the pipeline ids it needs;
  - `practices` = the book-wide set it exercises;
  - once authored, `practice_findings` passes, because U14 practises the 8.
  - It needs a `projects/` dir with `brief.ipynb` and `solutions.ipynb`; mirror the `python-projects` project
    layout and its tooling (structure-check, brief hygiene, project milestone headings — Phase A checks what the
    tooling requires for `kind: project`).

## Phases
- **Phase 0** — design amendment v6 (inline).
- **Phase A** — registry, syllabus rows, manifests, and the AST-guard roots (U14 and the capstone are covered by the
  existing global allowlist, so no change). Check the project-entry tooling requirements.
- **Phase B** — `build_models.py`, the ci-local wiring, `tests/test_unit14.py` (the required check plus gates

  feature family; build the ablation; a beyond-accuracy table; a cold-start slice; a session-log sequence readout.
- **Phase E** — the U14 milestone (Opus). It is shipped cleared and asserts gates (a), (d) and (e).
- **Phase F** — capstone `brief.ipynb` (Opus), then `solutions.ipynb` (a separate fresh Opus subagent), plus
  `teacher-notes.md` with a grading rubric.
- **Phase G** — U14 teacher notes (inline).
- **Phase H** — verification (named): `TMPDIR=/dev/shm bash scripts/ci-local.sh` ALL GREEN, plus
  `pre-merge-guard --pr`. Then report: the budget against the amended target; `practice_findings` passing with the
  capstone authored; the global test allowlist unchanged; cache determinism.
